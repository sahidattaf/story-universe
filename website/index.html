The Vision

Land, Legacy, Community

01

Land

120 hectares of untouched coastline on Curaçao's western edge — limestone cliffs, divi-divi trees, and water the color of old glass. Forty hectares remain wild, left to the flamingos and the iguanas, because some of the island isn't ours to build on.

02

Legacy

This land was nearly sold to a foreign developer before the family knew it could be theirs. What's being built here isn't a resort that happens to sit on the island — it's a continuation of a story that started with a deed, a notebook, and a father who refused to call it a legacy.

03

Community

A village that feels like the island: homes that breathe, a harbor shared by local and visiting boats, a hotel that opens the view instead of walling it off. Kai Kòrsou means House of Curaçao — built for the next generation, not just for now.
